# Melaiva brand foundation

## Name

**Melaiva** (meh-LAI-vah) combines *mela*—a gathering or fair—with *alive/viva*. It is intentionally broader than weddings so the product can serve social, cultural, and corporate celebrations.

Initial screening on 16 August 2026 found no obvious exact-name event or technology collision, and `melaiva.com` and `melaiva.in` appeared available. This is not legal clearance. Before paid acquisition, complete Indian trademark, company-name, social-handle, and domain checks, especially in Nice Classes 9, 35, 42, and 45 (and 36 if the company handles payment services).

## Positioning

Melaiva turns one clear celebration brief into a small set of marketplace-reviewed, comparable vendor offers, then helps the customer carry the chosen scope through booking and delivery.

Primary promise: **Your celebration, beautifully matched.**

Product line: **Plan once. Vendors compete. You celebrate.**

## Voice

- Warm and capable, never breathless.
- Clear about inclusions, exclusions, and uncertainty.
- India-aware without reducing the product to wedding clichés.
- Premium through restraint, not inflated claims.
- “AI-assisted” is a capability; it is not the brand promise.

## Visual system

- Aubergine `#3A193B`: trust, ceremony, primary surfaces.
- Marigold `#F2A900`: optimism, important actions, highlights.
- Peacock teal `#167D7F`: marketplace-reviewed states, planning intelligence.
- Rose `#E86A76`: warmth and secondary emphasis.
- Warm ivory `#FFF8EE`: the main canvas.
- Ink `#221B20`: readable body text.

Fraunces is the editorial display face; Manrope is the product and body face. Photography should feel candid, textural, contemporary, and recognizably Indian without appearing staged or costume-like.

## Trust rules

- Never publish invented customer, vendor, savings, or GMV figures.
- Use “Marketplace reviewed” for the operating approval state. Never imply KYC, government identity verification, business-registration verification, or a performance guarantee.
- Never imply that Melaiva independently provides regulated escrow.
- Use “protected milestone payments” only after integrating an appropriate licensed provider and obtaining legal review.
- Sponsored placement must be labelled and must not silently alter recommendation rankings.
